The ten UX moves

1 · Bottom tab bar

Home, Learn, Cards, Tutor, Reader — always one tap away. Hamburger keeps only Settings/Community/Messages/Admin. Today 4 of 5 core loops hide behind the menu.

2 · A real Home

“Today” answers “what should I do now?” with one primary action + goal ring. Landing directly in a review session is efficient but disorienting for returning users.

3 · One brand palette

Viridian/persimmon/gold replaces default Google-blue. Semantic colors (correct green, wrong red, grammar purple) stay separate from brand so feedback reads instantly.

4 · Display typeface

Bricolage Grotesque for headings, numbers and stats gives the app a voice; Public Sans keeps UI text quiet. Numbers use tabular figures everywhere they align.

5 · Focus mode for lessons

The player drops the tab bar entirely — progress, combo and a sticky action footer only. (The sticky footer is already shipped in this branch.)

6 · Consistent card language

16px radius, 1px hairline + soft shadow, press-down “3D” buttons for primary actions. Every surface — flashcard, deck, story, correction — is the same component family.

7 · Gamification in one place

Streak 🔥 and XP ★ live in the top strip on every tab, in brand colors (persimmon/gold), instead of varying pills per page.

8 · Interval previews on SRS buttons

Again/Hard/Good/Easy show when the card will return (1 min / 2 d / 4 d / 8 d) — the single most-requested Anki affordance.

9 · Trust lines for AI

“Knows your 214 words” under the tutor's name, “14 words known” on stories — surfacing WHY the AI content fits you builds confidence in it.

10 · Desktop rail + sidebar

Left rail navigation and a stats/tutor-nudge sidebar make desktop feel intentional instead of a stretched phone layout.
