Step 2 of 4 · Learn — 喺邊度

To ask where something is, put 喺邊度 (“at where”) after the thing you’re looking for — Cantonese keeps the question word in the answer’s position.

🔊
地鐵站dei6 tit3 zaam6喺hai2邊度bin1 dou6？
(lit. MTR-station at where?)
Where is the MTR station?
Quick check
Which one asks “Where is the bus stop?”
Right — the place word stays first. Ungraded warm-up: it never breaks your combo.
